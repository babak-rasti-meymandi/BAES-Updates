# BAES Research Proposal Submitted

**Date:** 2026-10-26  
**Status:** Submitted; outcome not yet known

A research proposal titled **“BAES: Human Authority and Authorized Action in AI-Assisted Engineering”** was submitted to the Foresight AI for Science & Safety Nodes program.

The proposed work concerns human authority and authorized action in AI-assisted engineering. Its intended contribution is to examine how explicit human authorization can be distinguished from AI execution, and how deviations from stated engineering intent can be identified and documented.

The public BAES reference is available at [BAES-Public](https://github.com/babak-rasti-meymandi/BAES-Public). The separate empirical project is maintained at [Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation](https://github.com/babak-rasti-meymandi/Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation).

## Status and limitations
