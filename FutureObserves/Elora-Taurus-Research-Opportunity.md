# BAES Research Opportunity Hand-over
## Elora Taurus — Potential Independent System for Future BAES Evaluation

**Status:** [Proposed] — Deferred Research Opportunity  
**Priority:** Low / Monitoring  
**Current BAES Priority:** Continue BAES stabilization, evidence preparation, and independent evaluation work.

## 1. Purpose

This hand-over records Elora Taurus as a potentially relevant independent engineering and research system for future examination under BAES.

The purpose is to preserve the research opportunity without initiating an evaluation, collaboration, endorsement, or change to BAES.

## 2. Initial Understanding

Based on publicly supplied project descriptions and development updates, Elora Taurus is an independent research and engineering initiative exploring non-neural and hybrid approaches to AI-like capabilities.

Its reported areas of development include:

- Symbolic and deterministic reasoning research.
- Governed execution and external AI Agent integration.
- Human review of proposed tasks and permissions.
- Task Ledger and execution evidence.
- Memory Atlas and correction-aware recall research.
- Runtime observability and resource governance.
- Local model experimentation and bounded execution environments.

These are preliminary descriptions of reported capabilities and research activities, not independently verified findings.

## 3. Potential Relevance to BAES

Elora may provide a practical system context in which selected BAES concepts could eventually be examined.

Potential areas of interest include:

- Human Intent and authorization boundaries.
- Human Authority versus AI execution authority.
- Delegated Execution and permission enforcement.
- Approval versus actual authorization.
- Evidence sufficiency and execution traceability.
- Human Override and permission revocation.
- Differences between declared governance mechanisms and demonstrated operational behaviour.

**Central research proposition:**

Elora Taurus may serve as an independently developed engineering system whose observable behaviour and execution evidence could be examined through a future BAES evaluation.

This proposition remains [Proposed], not [Established].

## 4. Important Research Boundaries

- Elora is not currently an approved BAES evaluation subject.
- No claim is made that Elora is compliant with BAES.
- No claim is made that its reported governance mechanisms are sufficient or effective.
- BAES must not be modified to accommodate Elora.
- Elora's architecture must not be treated as a source of authority for BAES concepts.
- No confidential BAES material may be disclosed.
- Public descriptions must be distinguished from independently verified implementation evidence.

## 5. Deferred Activities

The following activities are explicitly deferred:

1. Direct contact with Elora's developers.
2. Formal technical evaluation.
3. Comparative evaluation against BAES criteria.
4. Requests for private repositories, internal documentation, or execution access.
5. Research collaboration or joint publication.

## 6. Permitted Monitoring

Limited monitoring may include:

- Public technical documentation.
- Public repositories and release history.
- Published execution records and test reports.
- Changes to reported governance and Agent capabilities.
- Evidence of reproducibility and independently inspectable behaviour.

Monitoring must not displace current BAES priorities.

## 7. Conditions for Reconsideration

Reconsideration may occur when:

- BAES's current evidence and stabilization priorities have sufficiently progressed.
- Elora's implementation and public evidence are sufficiently accessible.
- A specific, bounded research question can be defined.
- Any potential evaluation can preserve BAES independence and avoid disclosure of confidential material.

## 8. Current Decision

**Decision: Record and defer.**

Elora Taurus is retained as a potential future evaluation context for BAES.

No implementation, collaboration, endorsement, or formal evaluation is authorized by this hand-over.

**BAES remains the primary research priority.**
