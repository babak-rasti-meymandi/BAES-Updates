# Research

![Evaluation and evidence](../assets/images/10-evaluation-evidence.svg)

This directory collects public research notes and materials associated with BAES-related questions. It complements, but does not replace, the dedicated repositories for the standard and specific evaluation projects.

## Current public evaluation project

The separate [Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation](https://github.com/babak-rasti-meymandi/Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation) project examines how reviewers distinguish stated human intent, semantic deviation, and evidence of authorization in a bounded synthetic engineering scenario.

- [Read the research brief](../collaboration/evaluation-brief.md) for the proposed question, method, schedule, deliverables, and indicative budget.
- The protocol and pre-registration plan remain drafts.
- The synthetic C case has an initial local test record, but no formal reviewer data collection or independent reviewer results are available yet.

## Possible contents

- research questions and scope statements;
- study or evaluation plans;
- public methods and test-design notes;
- summaries of findings, including limitations and unresolved questions;
- links to datasets, reports, papers, or reproducible artifacts that are intentionally public.

## Research integrity

Every research document should make its status clear. Separate hypotheses from observations, observations from interpretations, and interpretations from conclusions. Record limitations and avoid implying that a pilot or preliminary result establishes general validity.

Do not duplicate the full standard here. Do not publish confidential BAES material, restricted source material, or licensed content that cannot be redistributed.
