# BAES Updates

**Public information on BAES-related collaboration, sponsorship, consulting, and selected research activity.**

BAES-Updates is the public-facing space for selected collaboration opportunities, research activity, and development-related information associated with the Babak AI Engineering Standard (BAES).

It complements the standard's public reference. It does not replace BAES-Public or redefine the standard.

## Purpose

This repository provides public information about:

- founding sponsorship and research collaboration opportunities;
- the proposed Authority & Evidence consulting service;
- independent research scope, status, and supporting documents;
- selected development-related information intended for external discussion.

It is intended to make engagement routes clear while keeping the standard's definitions and architecture in their appropriate reference repository.

## Publication principles

1. **Accurate status:** distinguish proposed, active, deferred, completed, and independently verified work.
2. **Evidence before claims:** report methods, observations, limitations, and outcomes without overstating what they demonstrate.
3. **Research independence:** sponsorship or collaboration must not predetermine research findings or control BAES standardization decisions.
4. **Intellectual-property clarity:** ownership, licensing, permitted use, and any commercial rights must be defined separately in writing.
5. **Public-safe disclosure:** do not publish confidential material, private correspondence, credentials, or restricted project content.
6. **No implied endorsement:** references to external organizations, standards, or projects do not imply their endorsement of BAES.

## Current status

BAES-Updates describes proposed engagement routes and selected research activity. The existence of a proposal or consulting page does not imply secured funding, an existing partnership, completed independent validation, certification, or endorsement.

The BAES Foundation and its published status should be assessed through the relevant documents in [BAES-Public](https://github.com/babak-rasti-meymandi/BAES-Public).
