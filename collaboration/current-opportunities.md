# Current Research and Collaboration Opportunities

This page lists current opportunities to fund, evaluate, and collaborate on the Babak AI Engineering Standard (BAES).

Each opportunity is subject to a clearly defined scope, agreed deliverables, and transparent terms. Listing an opportunity does not imply an existing partnership, funding commitment, or endorsement.

## Opportunity 01 — Independent Evaluation of Human Authorization in AI-Assisted Work

**Status:** Open for Discussion  
**Proposed duration:** Six months  
**Indicative funding request:** USD 30,000

### Objective

Conduct an independent, evidence-based evaluation of how human intent, authorization, delegated AI actions, and human oversight can be represented and assessed in AI-assisted work.

The evaluation will use a defined research question and selected scenarios from an agreed scientific, engineering, or other relevant professional domain in which AI participates in consequential work.

The initial engagement will focus on a bounded evaluation rather than attempting to establish universal validity across all domains.

### Proposed Scope of Work

The project may include:

1. Defining the research question, evaluation boundaries, and authorization requirements.
2. Designing representative scenarios and explicit evaluation criteria.
3. Establishing evidence requirements and documenting the evaluation procedure.
4. Conducting the evaluation and recording observed outcomes, deviations, and uncertainties.
5. Analyzing results, limitations, and opportunities for further research.
6. Preparing a final report and an appropriate public summary.

The final scope will be agreed with the funding organization before work begins.

### Expected Deliverables

- **Research plan:** Research question, scope, assumptions, and evaluation design.
- **Evaluation materials:** Scenarios, criteria, and procedures appropriate to the agreed scope.
- **Evidence package:** Relevant observations, recorded results, and supporting evidence, subject to applicable confidentiality and access restrictions.
- **Analysis report:** Findings, deviations, uncertainties, limitations, and conclusions supported by the evidence.
- **Final report and public summary:** A documented account of the work, with reproducible materials published where appropriate and permitted.

The precise deliverables and acceptance criteria will be agreed in advance.

### Funding and Duration

The initial proposal is approximately **USD 30,000 for six months**.

This is an indicative amount, not a fixed quotation or an existing funding commitment. The final budget will depend on the agreed research question, scope, resources, access requirements, and deliverables.

A smaller or larger engagement may be considered if it supports a coherent and clearly bounded research objective.

### Collaboration Model

A funding or research partner may:

- Propose a relevant research question or application domain.
- Contribute domain expertise and feedback.
- Provide authorized access to appropriate systems, test environments, or evaluation materials.
- Review the agreed scope and deliverables.

Any access to systems or confidential information will require appropriate authorization and agreed handling conditions.

### Independence, Ownership, and Transparency

The evaluation must be conducted and reported according to the evidence obtained. Funding does not guarantee a favorable result or permit the sponsor to dictate research conclusions.

The engagement does not automatically transfer ownership of BAES or grant unrestricted rights to its use. Intellectual property, licensing, publication, confidentiality, and permitted use of project outputs must be agreed in writing before the engagement begins.

Public reporting will distinguish observed findings from assumptions, interpretations, and unresolved questions.

### Why Collaborate?

This engagement offers an opportunity to investigate practical questions about human authority and AI-assisted action through a bounded research project with documented methods, evidence, and limitations.

The value to a partner is the resulting research and evaluation work—not an advance guarantee that BAES or any evaluated system will achieve a particular outcome.

### How to Discuss This Opportunity

Organizations interested in funding, hosting, or contributing to this evaluation are invited to initiate a written discussion about the research question, scope, deliverables, budget, and terms.
