# Collaboration

This directory describes public areas where research discussion or collaboration related to BAES may be useful.

## Areas of interest

Potential discussions may include:

- independent evaluation of human authorization in AI-assisted engineering;
- identifying and documenting semantic deviation between stated intent and AI-produced changes;
- evaluation design, test scenarios, evidence quality, and traceability;
- comparisons with publicly available AI governance and risk-management materials, within applicable licensing limits;
- academic discussion, research supervision, and responsible publication of results.

These are areas of interest, not claims of an existing partnership or a request for any organization to endorse BAES.

## Ways to engage

Depending on the scope, useful forms of engagement may include research feedback, methodological discussion, independent review, academic collaboration, or support for a bounded evaluation. Any funding or partnership arrangement would need to be discussed explicitly; none is implied by this repository.

The project aims to make appropriate methods, test cases, and results public where feasible, while protecting confidential material and respecting third-party licensing restrictions.

## Contact and status

For now, use the public contact route listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile or open a discussion through the relevant public project repository. Do not treat a proposal or outreach message as evidence of acceptance, funding, or an established relationship.

When a specific opportunity becomes active, add a separate dated file here with its scope, status, and public contact route.
